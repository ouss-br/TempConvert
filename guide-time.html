Time Conversion Guide.

Time units include seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, and longer calendar-based periods. Seconds, minutes, and hours have fixed relationships.

Useful conversion relationships

1 minute = 60 seconds

1 hour = 3,600 seconds

Example: 1 day = 86,400 seconds

When you may need this conversion

Schedules, durations, work estimates, study sessions, and technical calculations.

Important detail

Months and years are not fixed-length calendar units. Use calendar-aware date calculations for exact deadlines.
