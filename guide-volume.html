Volume Conversion Guide.

Volume measures three-dimensional space. Litres and millilitres are metric units; US and Imperial gallons are different customary units.

Useful conversion relationships

1 L = 1,000 mL

1 US gal ≈ 3.785411784 L

Example: 1 Imperial gal ≈ 4.54609 L

When you may need this conversion

Recipes, fuel quantities, beverages, storage containers, and liquid measurements.

Important detail

Check whether a gallon is US or Imperial before converting.
