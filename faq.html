Frequently asked questions.

Straightforward answers about using the converter, how results are displayed, and what to keep in mind.

Is TempConvert free to use?

Yes. The converter is free and does not require an account or sign-up.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Open the website in a modern browser on a phone, tablet, or computer.

Are my entered values uploaded?

The conversion feature processes values locally in your browser. If you share a conversion link, the value and selected units can appear in that link.

How do I change categories?

Choose a category beside the converter. The available units and default selection update for that category.

How do I swap units?

Use the swap control between the two unit panels.

Why is my result rounded?

Results are formatted to a maximum of ten significant digits for readability. The displayed number may not show every digit of the internal calculation.

Are months and years exact time units?

No. Their lengths vary in calendars. The time category uses average durations, so those conversions are approximate.

Why are there US and Imperial gallons?

They are different units. One US gallon is about 3.785 litres; one Imperial gallon is about 4.546 litres.

How does fuel economy conversion work?

Litres per 100 kilometres is an inverse measure: lower is more efficient. Kilometres per litre and miles per gallon increase as efficiency improves.

Can I use results for medical or engineering decisions?

Do not use this tool as the sole authority for safety-critical, medical, legal, engineering, or laboratory decisions. Verify important calculations with the relevant professional standard.

Does TempConvert save conversion history?

No conversion history or account is currently provided. Theme and saved category preferences may be stored in browser local storage.
