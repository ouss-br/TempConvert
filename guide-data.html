Digital Storage Conversion Guide.

Digital storage is measured in bits and bytes and their larger multiples. A byte contains eight bits.

Useful conversion relationships

1 byte = 8 bits

1 kB = 1,000 bytes (decimal)

Example: 1 KiB = 1,024 bytes (binary)

When you may need this conversion

File sizes, memory, data plans, download sizes, and drive capacity.

Important detail

Decimal prefixes use powers of 1,000; binary prefixes use powers of 1,024. kB and KiB are not interchangeable.
