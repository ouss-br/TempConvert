A clearer way to convert.

TempConvert is a free online unit converter built to make everyday measurement conversions quick, understandable, and accessible.

Why we built TempConvert

Measurements appear everywhere: in recipes, schoolwork, travel, fitness, home projects, science, technology, and everyday decisions. Switching between measurement systems should not require complicated pages or mental arithmetic. TempConvert brings frequently used conversion categories into one focused tool.

What you can convert

The converter covers twelve categories: temperature, length, weight (mass), volume, area, speed, time, digital storage, pressure, energy, power, and fuel economy.

How it works

Most categories convert through a common reference unit using conversion factors in the website code. Temperature uses dedicated formulas because Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin have different zero points. Fuel economy also uses reciprocal formulas because lower litres per 100 kilometres means better efficiency.

Accuracy, rounding, and limitations

Results are formatted for readability and may be rounded to a maximum of ten significant digits. Some time conversions, such as average months and years, are approximations rather than calendar calculations. Fuel-economy conversions distinguish US and Imperial gallons. For safety-critical, medical, legal, engineering, or laboratory work, verify important results against an authoritative standard.

Privacy-minded by design

Conversion values are processed locally in your browser. The current site stores selected preferences, such as theme and saved category, in browser local storage. Read our Privacy Policy for details and future service changes.
