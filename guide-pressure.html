Pressure Conversion Guide.

Pressure is force applied over an area. Common units include pascals, kilopascals, bar, atmospheres, and psi.

Useful conversion relationships

1 kPa = 1,000 Pa

1 bar = 100,000 Pa

Example: 1 bar ≈ 14.5038 psi

When you may need this conversion

Tyre pressures, weather, engineering, industrial systems, and science.

Important detail

Check whether a specification is gauge pressure or absolute pressure; the unit alone does not tell you.
