Power Conversion Guide.

Power is the rate at which energy is transferred or work is done. It is not the same as energy.

Useful conversion relationships

1 kW = 1,000 W

1 mechanical hp ≈ 745.699872 W

Example: 100 hp ≈ 74.57 kW

When you may need this conversion

Motors, engines, appliances, generators, and electrical equipment.

Important detail

This converter uses mechanical horsepower. Metric horsepower (PS) is a different definition.
