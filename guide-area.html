Area Conversion Guide.

Area measures a two-dimensional surface. A square with sides of one metre has an area of one square metre.

Useful conversion relationships

1 m² ≈ 10.7639104 ft²

1 hectare = 10,000 m²

Example: 100 m² ≈ 1,076.391 ft²

When you may need this conversion

Floor plans, land sizes, painting, landscaping, and property descriptions.

Important detail

Square units scale in two dimensions; the square-metre to square-foot factor is about 10.764, not 3.281.
