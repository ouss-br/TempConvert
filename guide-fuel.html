Fuel Economy Conversion Guide.

Fuel economy describes how far a vehicle travels using a quantity of fuel. Some units measure fuel consumed per distance; others measure distance per volume.

Useful conversion relationships

km/L = 100 ÷ (L/100 km)

US mpg = 235.214583 ÷ (L/100 km)

Example: 8 L/100 km ≈ 29.4018 US mpg

When you may need this conversion

Comparing vehicle specifications from markets that use different units.

Important detail

L/100 km is inverse: lower is better. mpg is direct: higher is better. US and Imperial mpg differ.
