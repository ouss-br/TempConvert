Energy Conversion Guide.

Energy describes the capacity to do work or transfer heat. Joules are the SI unit; kWh and calories are common elsewhere.

Useful conversion relationships

1 kJ = 1,000 J

1 kWh = 3,600,000 J

Example: 1 kcal = 4,184 J

When you may need this conversion

Electricity bills, food labels, physics problems, appliances, and heating specifications.

Important detail

A food Calorie is commonly equivalent to one kilocalorie (kcal).
