Speed Conversion Guide.

Speed measures distance travelled per unit of time. Common units include km/h, mph, m/s, and knots.

Useful conversion relationships

1 m/s = 3.6 km/h

1 mph ≈ 1.609344 km/h

Example: 100 km/h ≈ 62.1371 mph

When you may need this conversion

Travel, vehicle specifications, and studying motion.

Important detail

Knots are nautical miles per hour and are commonly used in aviation and marine navigation.
