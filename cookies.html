Cookie Policy

This page explains cookies and similar browser technologies that may be used when you visit TempConvert.

The current converter uses browser local storage for interface preferences. The live hosting platform and contact form provider may use additional technical technologies. This policy must be checked against the deployed site and updated before analytics or advertising is enabled.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, pixels, tags, and device identifiers. They can support essential site functions, remember preferences, measure usage, or deliver advertising.

2. Technologies used by the current converter

The converter currently uses local storage to remember your preferred theme and saved conversion category. This is browser storage, not an account or a server-side profile. You can clear it through your browser’s site-data settings; doing so may reset those preferences.

3. Hosting and contact form technologies

The hosting provider may use technical logs or security technologies to deliver and protect the website. The contact form is routed through FormSubmit, which may use technical information and anti-abuse mechanisms to process submissions. Consult the relevant providers’ current documentation for their exact technologies and retention practices.

4. Analytics and advertising cookies

This release does not include Google Analytics or Google AdSense code. If analytics or advertising is added, third-party providers may store or access cookies or similar identifiers to measure usage, prevent fraud, or select and personalize advertisements. The owner must identify the actual providers and technologies and configure any required consent mechanism before activating them.

5. Your choices

You can control or delete cookies and site storage through your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect website functions. Where applicable law requires prior consent for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, the website must obtain valid consent before setting or accessing them; a browser setting alone may not satisfy all legal requirements.
