Length Conversion Guide.

Length describes distance or size in one dimension. Metric units use powers of ten; customary units include inches, feet, yards, and miles.

Useful conversion relationships

1 inch = 2.54 centimetres

1 mile = 1.609344 kilometres

Example: 5 km ≈ 3.10686 mi

When you may need this conversion

Travel distances, product dimensions, construction measurements, sports, and maps.

Important detail

For precision work, retain more decimal places than you need for the final display.
