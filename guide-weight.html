Weight and Mass Conversion Guide.

Everyday language often says weight when referring to mass. Common mass units include kilograms, grams, pounds, and ounces.

Useful conversion relationships

1 kg = 1,000 g

1 kg ≈ 2.20462262 lb

Example: 70 kg ≈ 154.3236 lb

When you may need this conversion

Cooking, luggage limits, shipping, fitness, and product specifications.

Important detail

Mass is not the same as force; physical weight depends on gravitational acceleration.
